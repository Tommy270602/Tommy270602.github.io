Research

My master's thesis, "Privacy Attacks and Countermeasures in Biometric Gait Identification", was supervised by Simone Milani at the University of Padova. It asks what an attacker can learn from a gait recognition model, and how to defend against it.

My wider research is on continual learning: models that recognise new people over time without retraining from scratch and without forgetting the ones they already know.
