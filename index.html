About

I'm a cybersecurity specialist at De'Longhi Group in Treviso and a master's student at the University of Padova.

My work sits between two fields that rarely talk to each other: securing systems inside a large industrial company, and building machine learning models that keep learning from human movement.

Research

My master's thesis, supervised by Simone Milani, is about continual learning for gait identification: a model that recognises new people over time without retraining from scratch and without forgetting the ones it already knows.

I also look at these models from a privacy angle, testing what an attacker could learn from them.
